Update the Stonegate Intelligence Group Client Portal and Billable Hours system with the following access structure.

IMPORTANT ACCESS RULE

Clients/customers MUST NOT have access to the internal Billable Hours page.

The Billable Hours page is strictly internal and should only be accessible to authorized Stonegate users.

Clients should never be able to see:

* Individual time entries
* Investigator names
* Internal notes
* Internal billing calculations
* Internal hourly tracking
* Unbilled time
* Internal billing rates unless intentionally included on a statement
* Other clients’ information
* Internal case-management information

Enforce this through backend authorization, not simply by hiding navigation links.

CLIENT PORTAL — BILLING STATEMENTS

Create a client-facing section in the Client Portal called:

“Billing Statements”

This is where Stonegate can publish completed monthly billing statements for the client.

The client should only see statements that have been specifically associated with their Client ID.

The administrator should be able to choose what information appears on the client-facing statement.

SECURITY

A client should only be able to access:

* Their own Client Portal
* Their own statements
* Their own documents
* Their own engagement information that Stonegate has chosen to expose

Do not rely on frontend navigation hiding.

Enforce authorization on every backend request.

A client must not be able to access another client’s statement by manually changing a URL, statement ID, client ID, or other parameter.

IMPORTANT

Keep the internal Billable Hours system completely separate from the client-facing Billing Statements interface.

The statement is a controlled, client-facing summary generated from internal billing information.

Do not expose the underlying internal time-entry records to the client.

After implementation, test both user types:

INTERNAL USER:
Can access Billable Hours and create/publish statements.

CLIENT:
Cannot access Billable Hours.
Can access only published Billing Statements belonging to that client.
